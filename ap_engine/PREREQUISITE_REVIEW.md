## Graph integration

graph.js accepts only resolved AND-only strict prerequisites. Do not flatten these records into prereqs arrays. OR choices, concurrent enrollment, unresolved rules, placement, aliases and supporting courses require a separate adapter.

See prerequisite_validation.json for the cycle-check results. Strict reference graphs combine OR branches for inspection and exclude concurrent links; they do not prove a valid semester schedule. External legacy/supporting records can still have unresearched prerequisites.
